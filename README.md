# 🌾 KishanMitre — Smart Crop Price & Mandi Intelligence

> **Sell at the right time. Sell at the right mandi.**

KishanMitre is a **farmer-first agricultural market intelligence platform** developed as a **team project for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH)**. The project focuses on helping farmers make smarter decisions about **when to sell their crops and where to sell them**.

The platform combines historical crop-price analysis, AI-based price forecasting, demand and market signals, mandi comparison, transport-cost estimation, anomaly alerts, and bilingual guidance into a single farmer-friendly dashboard.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://aesthetic-crepe-89c4ab.netlify.app/

## 🇮🇳 Smart India Hackathon (SIH)

KishanMitre was developed as a **team-based solution for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH)**, one of India's largest innovation and problem-solving platforms.

The project focuses on addressing a real-world agricultural challenge: **helping farmers make better crop-selling and mandi-selection decisions using technology and market intelligence.**

Our team worked together across **ideation, research, website development, content creation, presentation, testing, error fixing, workflow coordination, and solution development** to transform the proposed problem into a functional web-based prototype.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

Farmers often have to make important selling decisions without having access to understandable market intelligence. Simply looking at the highest mandi price may also be misleading because transportation costs can significantly reduce the actual profit.

**KishanMitre aims to solve this problem by helping farmers:**

- 📈 Understand historical crop-price and demand trends
- 🔮 Forecast possible short-term price movements
- 🏪 Compare different mandis
- 🚚 Account for transportation costs
- 💰 Estimate actual net returns
- ⚠️ Identify unusual market conditions
- 🌐 Access information in both English and Hindi
- 🤖 Get simple conversational guidance through an AI assistant

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 1. Interactive Market Dashboard

The main dashboard allows users to select:

- 🌱 Crop
- 📍 State
- 🗺️ District
- 🏪 Mandi
- 📅 Forecast horizon

After selecting the required information, the system generates a market insight package containing demand, arrivals, weather suitability, price trends, and market signals.

---

### 📈 2. Price & Demand Trend Analysis

KishanMitre provides an interactive view of historical market activity.

The platform includes:

- 90 days of historical data
- 📈 Price trends
- 📊 Demand trends
- Observed vs forecasted values
- Interactive graph updates based on selected inputs

This helps users understand how the market has been behaving before making a selling decision.

---

### 🔮 3. AI Price Forecasting

The **Price Predictor** provides short-term forecast horizons of:

- **7 days**
- **15 days**
- **30 days**

The system translates the forecast into simple farmer-oriented guidance such as:

- 🟢 **HOLD**
- 🟠 **SELL SOON**

It also provides a decision window and market alert to make the prediction easier to understand.

> ⚠️ The current deployment is a prototype, so the displayed prices, forecasts, and market signals are simulated for demonstration and are **not live trading advice**.

---

### 🏪 4. Cross-Mandi Comparison

One of the key features of KishanMitre is its **net-return based mandi comparison**.

Instead of recommending a mandi only because it has a higher gross price, the platform considers estimated transportation costs.
